LetterDraw Privacy Policy

Last updated: LAST-UPDATED-DATE

LetterDraw does not collect, store or share any personal data on its own servers. I don't run any servers for the app.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings (player name, haptics, sounds, default scoring mode), your game history, any game in progress, and your stats and progress on your device. It is not sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

Online play, leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. When you use them, Apple processes your Game Center identity, your display name, your friends list (if you choose to invite a friend), your matches, scores and achievements, under Apple's Privacy Policy. I don't receive your Game Center data other than what the app shows to you. Game Center is optional. You can play locally or against the computer without signing in.

The game state sent to your opponent in an online match contains the tiles and the words played. It doesn't contain your name or any other personal details.

What I don't do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No sale or sharing of data.

Children

LetterDraw is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
