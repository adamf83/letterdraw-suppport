LetterDraw Support

LetterDraw is a two-player word game. Take turns forming words from one shared pool of letter tiles. The highest total wins.

How to play

The rules

A game starts with 25 tiles. Players alternate turns. On your turn, tap tiles in order to spell a word, then tap Submit. A word needs at least 3 letters, must be in the dictionary, must not have been played already in that game, and must be buildable from the tiles still in the pool. Played tiles are removed and not replaced.

The game ends when no unused word can be built from the remaining tiles, or when a player ends it early. The highest total wins. Equal totals are a tie.

Scoring modes

Simple, Scrabble and Bonus modes are explained on the game setup screen and under Rules in the app.

I can't make a word. What now?

Use Skip turn, under Clear and Submit. A skip scores nothing and uses no tiles. Skipping online needs both players to be on a current version of the app.

Common questions

Online play doesn't work or I can't sign in

Online games use Apple's Game Center. Open Settings > Game Center on your iPhone or iPad and sign in with your Apple Account, then reopen LetterDraw. Check that you have an internet connection. Online play is optional: pass-and-play and playing against the computer work without an account or a connection.

My opponent's move isn't showing

Pull down on the match list in the online lobby to refresh. Game Center can take a short while to deliver turns, particularly if the other player's app is closed. Make sure notifications for LetterDraw are on in Settings.

What does Random opponent do?

Game Center finds a stranger for you. You play first, and Game Center only starts looking for an opponent after your first turn, so there may be a wait.

Can I play a friend who has an iPad?

Yes. iPhone and iPad players can be in the same match.

A word I think is valid was rejected

LetterDraw uses a public-domain word list plus some British spellings. It doesn't include every word, and it leaves out proper nouns and abbreviations. Email me the word and I'll look at it.

Where is my game history stored? How do I delete it?

On your device only. Open History to delete a game by swiping, or tap Clear history. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
